SCREEN 5 Onboarding — six steps, brand moment to a living Discover
Decisions
· Step 2 teaches the lens with the lens. Its three options are the actual glyphs — half-shaded circle for Anime, half-shaded for Manga, full circle for Both — so the first time you meet that icon you are choosing with it, not decoding it later in a top bar.
· One shared chrome on all six: 56 dp bar with Skip at the trailing edge, 6-step indicator above a single primary. Skip is present on every step, including the first — the flow promises skippable and a missing Skip on step 1 would break it before it starts.
· Step 5 has no primary button. Its three cards are the choice; a Continue underneath would make "which of these" and "go" compete. Start fresh is the third card, not a text link, so the honest option is not the quiet one.
· Manga DNA is loud on steps 1 and 6 and silent on 2–5: the two brand moments book-end four screens of plain forms. Halftone burst on 1, ink splash and speed sweep on 6.
· Step 4 lists official portals by name only, with no install control — per the legal position, nothing is bundled or pre-filled. The paste field is the only way content arrives.
Notes for you
· Blue per step: 1 → two (dot, Get started) · 2 → three (dot, selected option, Continue) · 3 → three (dot, checkboxes as one repeated system element, Continue) · 4 → two · 5 → one (dot only) · 6 → two.
· Arabic sits first in step 3 and is pre-checked alongside English, matching the rule that Arabic is prominent rather than alphabetically buried. The list is written in each language's own script.
· Step 6 lands on Discover, and says so out loud — the never-empty promise is the last thing you read before you see it.
Two values not in design.md: the 6-step indicator (6 dp dots, active 16 × 6 pill, 8 dp gap) and the 72 dp option row. Confirm and I will add them.
