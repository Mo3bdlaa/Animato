SCREEN 1 · v2 Home — lens is a top-bar icon, brand-sheet card treatment, white wordmark
What changed and why
· The lens left the canvas and became a top-bar icon, and the icon is the state: aperture = All, play = Anime, book = Manga. Filtered is an active state, so Anime/Manga render the glyph blue; All stays white. Tap opens a 184 dp menu with a check on the current lens — 2b.
· Your library is gone. Cards, then a hairline strip — neither earned 132 dp for four counts nobody acts on. Home is now the two things a session actually needs: what you were in the middle of, and what arrived since. The counts belong on Library, where the numbers are the content. All four stat rows now sit above the fold in its place.
· Killing the 56 dp chip row paid for bigger covers: 148 × 222 (2:3, r12) instead of 112 × 168. Rail geometry is 16 + 148 + 12 + 148 = 324, so two cards sit whole and the third is a 57 dp sliver starting at 336 — and the sliver deliberately carries no text overlay, because a partial card would chop its own title mid-word. Three whole cards is only possible at ≤ 112 dp; I chose the bigger cover.
· Continue cards now carry title + Ch. 254 · 2h ago on the cover over an ink scrim (brand sheet), progress flush to the bottom edge. That removes the 40 dp text stack under each card, so three sections plus three update rows sit above the fold instead of two.
· Update rows put the timestamp on the trailing edge (brand sheet), widening the title column from 200 to 236 dp.
· Top-bar wordmark is now the white/paper lockup, unrecoloured, 65 × 28.
Three things this forces you to decide
· design.md §Components says the lens is chips “identical on Home, Library, Discover, Updates, Search”. Your call overrides it — the icon has to replace those chips on all five screens, which leaves Library's category chips as the only chip row there. Confirm and I will carry it through and amend design.md.
· NEW pill: design.md says Accent #06B6D4, the brand sheet draws it blue. I kept Accent — blue is spoken for.
· Brand sheet labels the stat chips Reading / Watching; those are media words, banned in shared UI. I kept In progress / Completed / Downloaded / Tracking.
Blue count — 2a: two (progress, Home tab). 2b: three (lens glyph, progress, Home tab). 2c: two (Discover button, Home tab).
