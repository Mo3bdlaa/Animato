SCREEN 4 Title page — manga (chapters + range toolbar + scrubber) · anime (episodes)
Decisions
· Header is a 260 dp backdrop: the cover blurred and darkened behind a 112 × 168 cover at 16, with an Ink Black gradient carrying the text. Backdrop is the same art, not a second image to fetch.
· The primary is resume-aware — Resume · Ch. 184 / Resume · Ep. 12. One primary per screen, so heart / tracking / WebView / share are icon buttons, and the tracking ring is a 3 dp arc around its glyph, not a fifth pill.
· Tabs are About / Chapters / Tracking / Also on — Chapters is the landing tab, because the reason people come here is the next unread item.
· Manga gets the sticky range toolbar (184–160 ▾, unread filter, sort) and the fast scrubber on the right edge: 4 dp track, 44 dp thumb, tabular label. Anime does not — 24 episodes need no scrubber, and 1187 chapters do.
· Anime rows are 16:9 thumbs (96 × 54, r8) with a 3 dp progress bar on the watched one; manga rows are 40 dp numbered squares. Same row height (64), same trailing states.
Notes for you
· Blue budget — 6a three: Resume, the read-progress bar under the header, the active tab underline. Chapter rows use Success for read and Muted for unread; the unread count pill stays blue because that is the design.md cover-card rule carried inward.
· The heart is filled + white for In library. Never the word "favorite", per design.md.
· “Also on” lists other sources for the same title — the recovery feature the brand sheet calls Smart Recovery.
· 6c is preview mode, the state a Discover metadata title opens in: full info and tracking, but Chapters and Also on are disabled rather than hidden — hiding them would teach that this title has none, when the truth is that no installed source carries it yet. The primary becomes Find in your sources, which runs the global search; found → this page completes into 6a, found nowhere → the Search 13c dead-end with Add sources.
Two values design.md does not define: the 16:9 episode thumb (96 × 54, r8) and the scrubber (4 dp track, 44 dp thumb). Confirm or give me numbers.
