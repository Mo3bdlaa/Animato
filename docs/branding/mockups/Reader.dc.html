READER Manga reader — chrome visible · dark and Paper
Decisions
· No status bar and no bottom nav: the reader is immersive. Chrome is one tap away and sits over the page — 56 dp top bar, 104 dp bottom bar, both Surface, no elevation shadow.
· The page is fit-to-width: 393 × 590 at 2:3, centred in the 692 dp gap, so the letterbox is 51 dp top and bottom. Letterbox takes the background colour, never a third grey.
· The scrubber runs right-to-left — Japanese page order. 82% read means the thumb sits 18% from the left. This is the one control in the app whose direction does not follow the locale, it follows the book.
· Blue is the read-position fill and nothing else. Page count, brightness, mode and lock are Muted; the reading-mode row is icon-only because those four are muscle memory, not first-run discovery.
Paper variant
· design.md defines Paper #F2EEE5 and White #FFFFFF but no light surface, no light hairline. I used White for bars on Paper, Ink Black for primary text, Muted unchanged, and #08080C14 for hairlines and tracks — the mirror of #FFFFFF14. Those two need to become tokens; say the word and I will add them to design.md.
· Paper is the reading-comfort theme, so it is the one place the page letterbox is warm rather than black. Everything else keeps identical geometry — same 56 / 104 / 590, same blue.
Blue budget: two per artboard (read fill, and the filled bookmark that means this page is marked).
