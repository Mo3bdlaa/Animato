UX.md EXTRAS Tracking hub · Repositories — the two sub-screens the brief's eleven never covered
Tracking hub — 16a
· Signed-in and signed-out rows are the same height, and the difference is a caption plus one trailing control: Sync now when connected, Sign in when not. Two visual languages for one list would imply the disconnected trackers are broken rather than optional.
· Sync state is words, not a ring here — Synced 4m ago, 12 titles queued, Failed · token expired. The ring belongs on the title page, where there is one title and no room for a sentence.
· Per-title binding is deliberately absent: it lives on the title page's Tracking tab, where the title is. This screen is accounts and policy only.
· The activity feed is capped at four rows and dated in words, because its job is confidence — something happened, recently — not an audit log.
Repositories — 16b
· The trust prompt is inline and in plain words, not a modal: a repository is a stranger's list of code, so the sentence sits under the field, before you paste, where it can still change your mind.
· Added repositories show host and count, never the full URL — a 90-character URL ellipsised into a row tells you nothing. Tapping opens the detail with the full address.
· Official portals repeat here by name only, identical to onboarding step 4. Nothing is bundled or pre-filled.
Blue count: 16a one (Sync now, the only action in progress). 16b one (Add). Neither screen has a bottom bar — both are sub-screens, per design.md v1.1.
