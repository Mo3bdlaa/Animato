SCREEN 7 Settings root — ten entries · search · Reading and Watching as siblings
Decisions
· No section headers. Ten rows in the UX.md order, one flat list — grouping ten items into three buckets adds a second thing to read before the thing you came for. The order already is the grouping: look → shelf → the two media → where content comes from → what it costs → the outside world → your data → the machine.
· Reading and Watching sit adjacent, identical in height, weight and icon treatment. That symmetry is the entire point of the screen: neither medium is a sub-folder of the other. If one ever gains a badge or a count the other must too.
· Rows are 72 dp — 24 dp icon, title 15 Medium, subtitle 13 Muted saying what is inside, no chevron. A chevron on every row of a ten-row list is 10 identical arrows carrying no information; the whole row is the target.
· Search is a field, not an icon — 48 dp, Surface, full-round, directly under the title. UX.md says settings search spans everything; a field that is visible teaches that, a magnifier hides it.
· No blue on this screen at all — nothing here is active or in progress. 11b spends one, on the switch that is on. That is the honest reading of the blue rule: a settings list is inventory, not state.
Notes for you
· All ten subtitles name real contents, so this doubles as the migration map: Watching explicitly lists player, gestures, subtitles, decoders and external player — the settings that are buried inside today's “Anime” bucket. The word Anime as a settings bucket does not appear anywhere on this screen.
· 11b is one level down — Watching — included because the sibling claim is only provable if the child screens look alike. It is the M3 switch row, 72 dp, and the section labels there are present, because a leaf screen is a list of controls rather than destinations.
· Advanced · About is one row, not two: version, licences and logs are the same errand — the row people hit when something is wrong or a reviewer asks what version they have.
Two values not in design.md: the 72 dp settings row and the 48 dp search field. Both follow from the 4 dp grid and the 48 dp target minimum, but say the word and I will write them in.
