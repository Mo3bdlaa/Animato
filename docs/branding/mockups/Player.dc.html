PLAYER Anime player — portrait, controls visible · dark and Paper
Decisions
· Portrait, video 393 × 221 (16:9) pinned under the status bar rather than centred — that leaves a real panel below it, so pausing to pick the next episode does not mean leaving the player. Rotating to landscape is the immersive case and it is the same overlay, edge to edge.
· Controls over video stay white on a #08080C scrim in both themes. You cannot put Ink Black text on someone's episode. The theme changes the surround and the panel, not the overlay — that is the honest light variant for a player.
· Skip intro is an outlined secondary that appears only in its window; Next episode is the row below the scrubber, not a pill, because it is a destination and not a toggle.
· Up next reuses the Title-page episode row verbatim: 96 × 54 thumb r8, 15 Medium, 13 Muted, blue 3 dp progress on the one in progress.
Paper variant
· Same borrowed tokens as the reader: White bars on Paper, Ink Black text, #08080C14 hairlines and tracks. Both are missing from design.md — a light surface and a light hairline. Two tokens, one decision from you.
· The letterbox around a 16:9 video on Paper is #08080C, not Paper: bright bars beside dark footage is the one place light theme actively hurts. Video sits in ink, the app sits on paper.
Blue budget: two per artboard (played position, and the in-progress bar on Up next).
