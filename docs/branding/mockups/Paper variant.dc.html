PAPER Light variant — Home · Library · Settings, same geometry
The substitution, in full
· Ink Black #08080C → Paper #F2EEE5 · Surface #151516 → Surface Light #FFFFFF · White text → Ink Text #08080C · hairlines and tracks #FFFFFF14 → #08080C14. Muted #9A9690, blue, Accent, Success and Warning are unchanged — Muted was chosen warm enough to read on both grounds, which is why Paper needs no second secondary.
· Not one dp moves. Same 56 dp bars, 148 × 222 Continue cards, 112 grid, 72 dp settings rows, 80 dp nav. If a light mock needed different spacing the palette would be doing structural work it has no business doing.
· Cards need their border back. On dark, #151516 separates from #08080C by luminance alone; White on Paper is only 4% apart, so the 1 dp #08080C14 inset border is load-bearing here rather than decorative.
· The wordmark switches to the blue-on-light lockup — unrecoloured, just the other supplied file.
Notes
· Blue count is identical to each screen's dark twin: 17a two, 17b two, 17c zero. Paper is a ground swap, not a licence to spend more colour.
· Cover placeholders sit on White with the same Muted glyph at higher opacity (0.6 vs 0.4) — a 40%-opacity grey line vanishes on Paper.
· Status-bar glyphs and the scrim over cover art invert too: on Paper the Continue card's text scrim stays ink, because it sits over artwork, not over the page. Same rule as the player's letterbox.
