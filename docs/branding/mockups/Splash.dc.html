SCREEN 11 Splash — Ink Black · Paper · what it replaces
Decisions
· The panel is gone. Your old splash showed the app icon — a rounded square with a frame and a halftone fill — floating on black, which reads as a sticker of the logo rather than the logo. design.md says centred unframed wordmark, no panel, no spinner. This is that.
· Wordmark is 240 dp wide, optically centred: the lockup sits 24 dp above true centre, because アニマト and the speed lines carry visual weight below it. Dead-centre maths looks low.
· アニマト is live text in Noto Sans JP 500, 22 dp, tracked +0.32em — not part of the PNG. That keeps it crisp at any density and matches the brand sheet's "never remove the Japanese text" rule.
· Speed lines: 8 strokes, 2 dp, Muted at 0.18–0.5 opacity, converging left to right. Splash is one of the four places design.md allows manga DNA — and the only one where it can be the whole composition.
· No spinner, no progress, no version string. Cold start is under a second; a spinner would advertise a wait that is not there.
Notes for you
· 9a is the default identity (Ink Black, blue wordmark). 9b is the Paper variant — same geometry, the light lockup, halftone at 0.10 because dots read heavier on Paper.
· The old icon-on-black splash is deleted, not archived — 9a replaces it.
· Blue budget: one. The wordmark. Nothing else on the screen is blue, which is the strongest possible statement of what blue means in this app.
· The tagline is not on the splash — it belongs to onboarding screen 1, where there is time to read it.
